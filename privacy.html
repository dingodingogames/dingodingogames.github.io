HEXAFLOW Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026 · Applies to the HEXAFLOW game for Android (com.dingodingo.hexaflow)

HEXAFLOW is a puzzle game made by dingodingo. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. We keep it to what the game needs to work, to save your progress, to run clans, and to show ads.

1

Information we collect

  • A random player ID. When you first open the game, you're signed in anonymously and given a random ID. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number or a password.
  • Game progress. Your level, stars, coins, gems, boosters, unlocked items and settings, saved to the cloud so your progress isn't lost.
  • Clan information, if you use clans: the player name and picture you choose in the game, your level, the clan you join or create (its name, description and picture), your quick-chat messages, lives you send and ask for, your weekly clan progress, and any reports you make about a clan. Clan chat only uses ready-made phrases; you can't type free text into chat.
  • Google Play Games, if you use it: when you're signed in to Play Games on your device, the game reports the achievements you earn and your leaderboard scores (levels cleared, best win streak, longest chain) to Google Play Games, linked to your Play Games profile. We don't receive your email address or contacts.
  • Purchases. If you buy something, Google Play handles the payment. We receive confirmation of what was bought, not your card or bank details.
  • Gameplay analytics, only if you agree in the game: events such as starting, winning or failing a level, which help us balance the game.
  • Advertising information. Our ad partner, Google AdMob, collects device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device type and ad interactions in order to show and measure ads.
  • Feedback you send us. If you use "Send feedback" in Settings, your email app sends us your message along with the game version, your level, your device model and your player ID, so we can look into it.
2

How we use it

  • To run the game and save and restore your progress.
  • To run clans: membership, chat, lives, weekly goals and rewards.
  • To show achievements and leaderboards through Google Play Games.
  • To deliver what you buy and restore purchases.
  • To show ads, limit how often they appear, and keep them out of your way after you make a purchase.
  • To understand how the game is played and improve it (only with your permission).
  • To answer feedback and support requests.
  • To keep clans safe, including reviewing reports and removing rude clan names.

We don't sell your personal information.

3

Services we use

These companies process information on our behalf or to provide their service, under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies): anonymous sign-in, cloud saves, clan servers and, with your permission, analytics. Unity's privacy policy
  • Google AdMob: ads. How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Play and Google Play Games Services: downloads, purchases, payments, achievements and leaderboards. Google's privacy policy
4

Your choices

  • Ad consent. Where the law requires it, such as in the UK, EU and some US states, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Ad personalization. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.
  • Analytics. Gameplay analytics only run if you say yes in the game, and you can turn them off in Settings.
  • Play Games. You can sign out of Play Games or change your Play Games profile visibility in the Google Play Games settings on your device.
  • No Ads. Buying No Ads removes ads between levels. Rewarded videos stay optional.
  • Notifications. You can turn game notifications off in Settings or in your device settings.
5

Deleting your HEXAFLOW data

You can have your HEXAFLOW data deleted at any time, by dingodingo, in two ways.

Delete your progress yourself, right away:

  1. Open HEXAFLOW and tap the Settings gear.
  2. Scroll down and tap Reset progress, then confirm.

This deletes your levels, stars, coins, gems, items and settings from your device and from our cloud save.

Ask us to delete everything:

  1. In HEXAFLOW, open Settings and copy the Player ID shown near the bottom.
  2. Email contact.dingodingo@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my HEXAFLOW data" and paste your Player ID.

Within 30 days we delete everything linked to that Player ID: your cloud save, your clan membership and clan records, your quick-chat messages, lives you sent or received, your block list, and reports you made. If you lead a clan, leadership passes to another member, or the clan is closed if you're the last one.

What may be kept: purchase records are kept by Google Play, as their policy requires. Achievements and leaderboard scores are held by Google Play Games, and you can manage them there. Anonymous, aggregated gameplay statistics that can't identify you may be kept. Ad data is handled by Google under its own policy, and you can reset your advertising ID in your device settings. Clan messages are only ever kept as a short recent history, and older ones are removed automatically.

6

Children

HEXAFLOW is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

7

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections and stored by the services listed above. Clan data can only be changed through our game servers, which check every request. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. Big changes will also be mentioned in the game.
